# Viraholic Companion — Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 2026-06-21_

Viraholic Companion is a browser extension that helps you draft replies for **your own**
X (Twitter) and LinkedIn account. This policy explains what it accesses and why.

## What the extension accesses

- **Content from the feed you are signed in to.** When you are on x.com or
  linkedin.com, the extension reads posts visible to your logged-in session so it can
  draft replies. It uses **your existing session** — it never asks for or stores your
  X/LinkedIn password.
- **Posts and profiles you choose to track.** Accounts and posts you explicitly add in
  the dashboard.
- **Drafts you create.** Reply text generated for your review.
- **Your AI API key**, if you provide one, so the extension can call the AI model.

## How your data is used

- Post text you choose to act on is sent to an **AI provider (Google Gemini / DeepSeek)**
  to generate a draft reply. No other third parties receive your content.
- Drafts and tracked items are stored **locally in your browser**
  (`chrome.storage.local` / `localStorage`) and synced to your own Viraholic dashboard.
- **Nothing is posted without your explicit approval.** The extension queues drafts and
  waits for you to review and confirm before any reply is sent. It includes a dry-run
  mode that drafts without posting.

## What the extension does NOT do

- Does not collect passwords or login credentials.
- Does not sell, rent, or share your data with advertisers or data brokers.
- Does not post, like, follow, or message automatically without your confirmation.
- Does not run on sites other than x.com, linkedin.com, and your own Viraholic dashboard.

## Data retention & control

Data lives in your browser and your dashboard. Removing the extension and clearing
the dashboard's local storage deletes it. You can revoke the AI API key at any time.
