FAQ

Questions, answered.

Will this get my X / LinkedIn account banned? ▾

No. The agent uses a stealth browser extension that mimics human browsing — natural scrolling, randomised timing, session breaks, captcha detection. It never auto-posts on your behalf. You always press Send.

Where does my data live? ▾

Everything is stored locally in your browser. The only data leaving your machine is the prompts you send to the Gemini API for content generation — same as if you were using ChatGPT yourself.

Do I need a Gemini API key? ▾

On the Solo plan, yes — free at aistudio.google.com/app/apikey. On the Founder plan we provide managed capacity, no key required.

Does it actually sound like me? ▾

A 7-question Voice Match quiz calibrates a voice profile across six dimensions — authority, energy, vulnerability, provocation, specificity, intimacy — plus rhythm and hook architecture. You can paste a writing sample for the AI to fine-tune against. The output is dramatically closer to your voice than a one-shot ChatGPT reply.

What if I'm not technical? ▾

You install the extension and fill in one form about your product. That's the technical bar. The agent does the rest.

How is this different from ChatGPT or Copy.ai? ▾

ChatGPT writes content on demand. Viraholic tells you who to write to — it scans LinkedIn, X, and Reddit and surfaces the exact accounts your buyers follow, scored and ranked. It also knows your voice, your product, your audience, your strategy, and your competitors. Everything stays in context.
