FAQ

The objections, handled.

How is this different from a cold-DM tool? ▾

Polar opposite. Cold tools shove you into strangers' inboxes and burn your reputation at a 1–3% reply rate. This makes you known first — useful replies and posts in the rooms your buyers already watch — so people reply because they recognize you. Inbound by reputation, not outbound by interruption.

Will this get my LinkedIn or X account banned? ▾

No. The agent never mass-blasts and never auto-posts: every draft waits for your one-tap approval, then publishes one at a time at a human pace. That's the exact pattern the platforms reward — useful replies on real conversations — and the opposite of what triggers a ban.

How fast will I see visibility move? ▾

Impressions move in week one — a reply under a big post puts you in front of its whole audience instantly. Profile visits and follows build over the first 2–4 active weeks. Inbound DMs are the lagging indicator and ride on your offer. The dashboard tracks all of it.

Does it really sound like me — or like ChatGPT? ▾

Like you. A short Voice Match quiz calibrates tone, energy, vulnerability, specificity, and rhythm — and you can paste a writing sample to fine-tune. Every draft is editable before it posts. Generic AI replies kill the trust that makes visibility convert, so sounding human is the whole design.

Do I lose control? Does it post on its own? ▾

Never without you. The agent finds, drafts, and queues. Nothing publishes until you read it and tap Approve — and you can edit any word first. Human-in-the-loop is the whole point.

What do I need for the agent to work? ▾

Be signed in to your X and LinkedIn accounts in Chrome, and install the Viraholic Chrome extension. The agent works through your own logged-in session — that's how it reads your feed and posts your approved replies under your name.
